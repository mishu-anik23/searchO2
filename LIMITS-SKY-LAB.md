# LIMITS — what this app does NOT claim

1. **Does not prove** that astrology predicts life outcomes.
2. Ephemeris accuracy is **classroom-grade**, not arcsecond JPL Horizons.
3. Placidus is **simplified**, not a certified production house engine.
4. Prediction Lab demos are **sanity / null checks**; import of user CSV cohorts is future work.
5. Symbolic keywords are **cultural**, not physical causes.
6. Tropical signs ≠ constellations; precession offset is shown deliberately.
