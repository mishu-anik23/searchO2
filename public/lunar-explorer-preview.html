Shackleton Crater

89.9°S, 0.0°E · South Pole Region
📖 Lunar Reconnaissance & Scientific Geography

A 21 km wide, 4.2 km deep impact crater sitting directly on the Lunar South Pole.

💡 Why It Matters for Science & Human Settlement

Sunlight never reaches the crater floor, keeping temperatures at -246°C where water ice survives for billions of years.

🎒 For Young Lunar Explorers (Kid-Friendly Summary)

A giant frozen refrigerator at the bottom of the Moon where sunlight never shines!
