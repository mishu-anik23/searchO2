Alpenhof Cheese Fermentation & Affinage Science

Curd formation is an enzymatic and microbial cascade: lactic acid bacteria ferment lactose into lactic acid, dropping milk pH to 4.6. Chymosin enzymatically cleaves kappa-casein micelle stabilizing hairs, causing calcium paracaseinate precipitation into an elastic matrix that traps cream fat globules while expelling whey.

🧪 Active Starter Cultures: Lactococcus lactis ssp. cremoris
📉 Isoelectric pH Drop: pH 6.65 → 4.62 (Casein Curdling)
🔪 Cutting Harp Wire Matrix: 0.8 mm High-Tensile 316L Wire Spacing
⚙️ Pneumatic Press Pressure: 4.2 Bar (Expelling 85% Free Moisture)
🌊 Affinage Saturated Brine: 20.5% NaCl at 11°C (Rind Formation)
🪵 Alpine Spruce Ripening: 12°C / 85% RH (Brevibacterium linens)
