# HQ · Flight Crew Avatars

## What was added
- `src/game/crew.ts` — roster (commander, pilots, systems, science, medical)
- HQ **Mission roster** panel with SVG flight-suit avatars
- Hire once (program funds) · assign up to **3 seats** for Crewmark
- Hover a card for the science / ops fact
- Unlock more crew after missions complete
- Planner shows seated names when Crewmark is selected
- Persisted in localStorage (`crewHired`, `crewSeats`)

## How to use
1. Open **HQ** after briefing
2. Hire crew (or use free commander seat)
3. **Assign seat** up to 3
4. **Plan a mission** → choose Crewmark-3 → seats listed on the bill panel
