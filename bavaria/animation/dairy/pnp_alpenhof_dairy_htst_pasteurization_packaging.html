Alpenhof HTST Pasteurization & QA Architecture

High-Temperature Short-Time (HTST) pasteurization targets complete thermal destruction of human pathogens (including heat-resistant Coxiella burnetii and Listeria monocytogenes) while preserving bio-available calcium, lactoglobulins, and native alpine milk flavor.

🔥 Thermal Setpoint: 72.0°C (161.6°F) for 15.0s
⚙️ 2-Stage Homogenization: 160 bar (Stage 1) / 40 bar (Stage 2)
🔬 Fat Globule Dispersion: Reduced from 4.0 µm to < 0.8 µm
🌱 Regenerative Efficiency: 92.4% Thermal Energy Recovery
🧪 Alkaline Phosphatase Test: Negative (Zero Active Enzyme)
📦 Aseptic Form-Fill-Seal: H2O2 Vapor Sterilization + Ultrasonic
❄️ Extended Cold-Chain Life: 21 Days at 4°C Constant
